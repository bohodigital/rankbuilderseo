## Dataset and period

Zero-click search is rising, but the 68% headline is easy to abuse. It includes searches that end on Google, sessions that stop, and follow-up behavior under the study’s methodology. It does not mean 68% of your rankings are worthless.

A June 2026 report based on SparkToro research using Similarweb clickstream data said **68.01% of U.S. Google searches ended without an external click** during the first four months of 2026.

The same report compared that with **60.45% in 2024**.

That is a substantial change.

It does not mean:

- 68% of all Google searches show an AI Overview;
- 68% of your site’s impressions are worthless;
- 68% of users got every answer from Google;
- organic SEO has lost 68% of its value;
- every industry has the same zero-click rate.

It means the study’s observed search journeys increasingly ended without a click leaving Google.

**What counts as zero-click?**

A zero-click search generally means the user performed a search but did not click through to an external website within the study’s defined journey.

Possible outcomes include:

- reading information directly on Google;
- using Maps;
- viewing a knowledge panel;
- seeing an AI Overview;
- running another Google search;
- ending the session;
- using a Google-owned surface.

The exact classification depends on the research methodology.

Do not turn “zero-click” into “the user learned nothing from publishers.”

Google’s result page itself can be built from information supplied by web sources.

**Why zero-click behavior is increasing.**

Several forces stack together.

**AI Overviews.**

Google can answer complex informational questions directly.

**AI Mode.**

Users can continue the research conversation inside Search.

**Featured snippets.**

These have answered simple questions for years.

**Knowledge panels.**

Entity facts appear without an external visit.

**Local packs.**

People can call, get directions, or check hours from Google.

**Shopping.**

Price, availability, products, and reviews can surface directly.

Zero-click behavior predates generative AI.

AI expands the amount of work the search page can finish itself.

## Methodology

**Follow-up searches matter.**

The 2026 reporting noted that the share of searches leading to another Google search also increased.

That is an important behavioral shift.

A user can perform:

```text
best CRM for contractors
→ CRM pricing
→ CRM reviews
→ company login
```

without leaving Google until later in the journey.

One query may not create a click.

The sequence can still influence a purchase.

SEO measurement needs to move from one-query attribution toward journey influence.

**AI Overviews and zero-click are related, not identical.**

Not every zero-click search has an AI Overview.

Not every AI Overview search is zero-click.

Pew research separately found users were less likely to click results when AI summaries appeared, while some users still clicked traditional or cited links.

Do not use the 68% figure as an AI Overview adoption statistic.

It is broader.

**Brand searches become more valuable.**

When informational answers are increasingly satisfied on the result page, one durable outcome becomes:

```text
user remembers brand
→ later searches brand directly
```

Track:

- branded impressions;
- branded clicks;
- direct traffic;
- returning users;
- assisted conversions.

A research article may influence a later branded query even if its original query did not produce a click.

That is difficult to attribute perfectly.

It is still worth measuring.

**Conversion intent still creates clicks.**

Many tasks require leaving the result page.

Examples:

- buy;
- book;
- apply;
- upload;
- download;
- login;
- contact;
- configure;
- use calculator;
- read original document.

This is why useful tools, products, and primary sources remain valuable.

A generated summary can explain mortgage amortization.

A calculator still needs an interactive interface.

## Result

**Build pages with a reason to visit.**

Weak informational page:

> A canonical tag tells search engines which URL is preferred.

The answer can be summarized in one sentence.

Stronger page:

- exact examples;
- troubleshooting tool;
- edge cases;
- screenshots;
- current Search Console behavior;
- decision tree;
- test script.

The goal is not to withhold the answer.

Give the direct answer, then provide depth that a summary cannot replace.

**Measure impression value.**

A ranking can matter without producing the historical click rate.

Track:

```text
impressions
CTR
brand searches
conversions
returning users
assisted revenue
citations
direct traffic
```

Do not celebrate impression growth automatically.

But do not treat every impression without a click as zero value either.

**Separate query classes.**

Build cohorts.

**Navigational.**

```text
brand login
brand pricing
brand support
```

**Transactional.**

```text
buy
quote
book
hire
```

**Comparative.**

```text
best
vs
alternative
review
```

**Informational.**

```text
what
how
why
```

Zero-click risk is not evenly distributed.

Question-heavy informational queries tend to encounter more direct-answer features.

**Change content mix deliberately.**

A site overdependent on commodity informational queries should diversify toward:

- tools;
- first-party research;
- comparisons;
- local utility;
- products;
- original reporting;
- community;
- email;
- direct audience.

This is not abandoning informational SEO.

It is reducing dependence on a click pattern Google no longer promises.

## Limitations

**FAQ.**

**What percentage of Google searches are zero-click in 2026?**

One widely reported SparkToro/Similarweb analysis put U.S. zero-click searches at 68.01% for the first four months of 2026.

**Does that mean SEO is dead?**

No. Search still influences discovery, branding, purchases, navigation, and conversion.

**Are AI Overviews responsible for all zero-click searches?**

No.

**Should I stop writing informational content?**

No. Create informational content when it serves your audience and supports a broader business or editorial purpose.

**What should replace CTR as a KPI?**

Nothing replaces CTR entirely. Add brand demand, conversion, citations, returning users, and assisted outcomes.

**2026 reporting checklist.**

- Query classes segmented.
- CTR tracked over time.
- AI feature exposure tracked.
- Branded demand tracked.
- Conversion tracked.
- Direct and returning traffic tracked.
- External benchmarks labeled.
- Industry differences considered.
- No 68% universalization.
- Content mix reviewed quarterly.

**Verdict.**

Zero-click search is not a rumor anymore. It is a structural measurement problem.

The correct response is not to stop doing SEO.

It is to stop pretending that **ranking, click, and business value are the same metric**.
