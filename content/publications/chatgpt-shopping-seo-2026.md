## Definition

ChatGPT Shopping does not publish a traditional merchant ranking formula. OpenAI does explain the inputs: user intent, product metadata, price, reviews, availability, quality, merchant status, and current product information. Merchants should optimize the data, not invent a 'ChatGPT keyword density.'

ChatGPT can show product results when a conversation has shopping intent.

OpenAI’s current shopping documentation says products are selected independently by ChatGPT and are **not ads** or influenced by OpenAI partnerships.[@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-1]

Product relevance can consider:

- the user’s query;
- conversation context;
- available options;
- price;
- reviews;
- ease of use;
- structured metadata from first-party and third-party providers;
- other relevant product information.

When a user opens a product, ChatGPT can also rank available merchants using factors such as:

- availability;
- price;
- quality;
- whether the merchant is the maker or primary seller.[@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-1]

This is not a traditional search ranking formula.

It is a product-discovery system shaped by the shopper’s stated needs.

**Why this is one of the hottest ecommerce queries.**

OpenAI expanded ChatGPT shopping significantly in March 2026.

The updated experience supports:

- visual browsing;
- side-by-side comparison;
- image-based product discovery;
- conversational refinement;
- current price and product detail;
- merchant product feeds through the Agentic Commerce Protocol.

More people are beginning product research inside an AI conversation instead of starting with a category page or ten blue links.

That makes the merchant question obvious:

> How do I make my products understandable and relevant to ChatGPT?

The answer begins with product data.

**User intent is richer than one keyword.**

Classic ecommerce keyword:

```text
women's running shoes
```

Conversational shopping query:

```text
I run 20 miles a week, overpronate a little, hate stiff shoes, and want something under $140 that works for wide feet.
```

The product needs enough information to be evaluated against several constraints:

- use case;
- gait;
- feel;
- price;
- width;
- activity level.

A feed containing only:

```text
Shoe Model X
$129
```

provides weaker matching information than a complete product record.

## Mechanism

**Product metadata matters.**

OpenAI says ChatGPT can consider structured metadata from merchants and third-party providers.[@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-1]

Useful product facts include:

- title;
- description;
- price;
- variants;
- availability;
- images;
- specifications;
- brand;
- size;
- color;
- material;
- features.

Do not interpret this as permission to stuff descriptions.

Write product information that distinguishes the item.

Weak:

> Best premium comfortable stylish high-quality running shoe for runners.

Strong:

> Neutral daily trainer with a 34 mm heel stack, wide-size options, removable insole, and breathable engineered mesh.

The second description contains actual decision information.

**Price is contextual.**

OpenAI says price can matter more when the user states a budget.[@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-1]

That seems obvious, but it changes optimization priorities.

If your product feed shows:

```text
$99
```

while the landing page now shows:

```text
$129
```

the product can be mismatched to a user constraint.

Keep price data current.

Also maintain:

- sale price;
- currency;
- variant price;
- shipping cost where supplied;
- promotions where supported.

Stale commerce data is relevance damage.

**Reviews can influence selection.**

OpenAI says reviews can be one of the considerations in shopping results.[@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-1]

ChatGPT can also display model-generated summaries based on reviews from public websites.

OpenAI notes that those reviews and ratings are not verified by OpenAI.

Merchants should therefore focus on genuine review quality.

Do not buy fake reviews to manipulate AI shopping.

That creates:

- regulatory risk;
- platform risk;
- reputation risk;
- bad training data for every system that reads the web.

**Product labels are not guarantees.**

ChatGPT can generate labels such as:

```text
Budget-friendly
Most popular
```

based on available information.[@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-1]

OpenAI explicitly says these are not guarantees or verified statements and may not reflect all market data.

A merchant should not advertise:

> ChatGPT certified us as the cheapest.

That is not what the label means.

**Merchant selection is a second layer.**

Product selection and merchant selection are separate problems.

Suppose the user selects one known vacuum model.

ChatGPT may show several merchants.

OpenAI says merchant ranking can consider:

- availability;
- price;
- quality;
- maker or primary-seller status.

So merchants need accurate offer data, not just strong product content.

**Availability needs to be real.**

A product that appears in stock but fails after the click creates a poor experience.

Synchronize:

- inventory;
- variant availability;
- regional stock;
- discontinued status;
- preorder status.

If feeds and website inventory disagree, fix the data architecture.

Do not “SEO” an out-of-stock item into appearing available.

## Examples

**Product images matter.**

ChatGPT shopping is visually rich.

Use:

- clean product images;
- alternate angles;
- correct variant image;
- useful scale;
- real colors;
- high resolution.

Image-based shopping also means visual similarity can become part of discovery.

A merchant using one tiny generic image loses useful product information.

**Shopify merchants.**

OpenAI says product data for Shopify merchants is already integrated through Shopify Catalog, helping products appear more accurately and completely in relevant conversations.[@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-1]

OpenAI says individual Shopify merchants do not need additional work for that integration.

That does not mean Shopify guarantees appearance.

Merchants still need accurate catalog data.

**Direct feeds.**

OpenAI also allows eligible merchants to provide direct product feeds.

That gives merchants another way to keep product information current.

This is especially relevant for:

- large catalogs;
- frequent price changes;
- inventory movement;
- variant-heavy products.

Feed submission is not a paid ranking placement.

Product results remain separate from ads under OpenAI’s current documentation.

**Ads and shopping results are different.**

OpenAI explicitly says ads are separate from shopping research and product results.[@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-1][@rb-algo-trend-06-15-source-2]

A merchant can participate in advertising and organic product discovery at the same time.

Do not report:

```text
ChatGPT shopping visibility
```

as one blended metric if one appearance was sponsored and another was product discovery.

Preserve channel identity.

**Product page SEO still matters.**

ChatGPT can use public product information in addition to merchant feeds.

Keep the landing page:

- crawlable;
- canonical;
- current;
- mobile usable;
- descriptive;
- consistent with the feed.

A feed promising one specification while the product page says another creates ambiguity.

The web page remains the final purchase and verification destination for many flows.

## Boundaries

**A ChatGPT shopping optimization checklist.**

- Product title specific.
- Description factual.
- Price current.
- Availability current.
- Variants complete.
- Brand accurate.
- Specifications complete.
- Images high quality.
- Reviews genuine.
- Landing URL stable.
- Feed and page consistent.
- Shopify Catalog status understood.
- Direct-feed eligibility reviewed.
- Ads measured separately.

**FAQ.**

**Are ChatGPT product results ads?**

OpenAI says product results are selected independently and are not ads.

**Does OpenAI publish a product ranking formula?**

No complete deterministic ranking formula is published. OpenAI describes relevant inputs and context.

**Do reviews affect product results?**

OpenAI says reviews can be considered depending on the user’s needs.

**Do Shopify stores need a direct feed?**

OpenAI says Shopify product data is already integrated through Shopify Catalog and individual merchants do not need additional work for that integration.

**Does paying for ChatGPT ads improve product ranking?**

OpenAI says ads are separate from product results.

**Verdict.**

ChatGPT Shopping SEO is primarily **catalog relevance engineering**.

Give the system accurate information about what the product is, who it is for, what it costs, whether it is available, and why it differs.

The useful optimization is better product truth, which is offensively less glamorous than inventing a new acronym.
