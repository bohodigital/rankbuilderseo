## Preconditions

Google Search Console can now measure how Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube posts perform in Google Search and Discover through new platform properties. That turns social search visibility from screenshot archaeology into a first-party measurement workflow.

Google announced **Search Console platform properties** on July 7, 2026.[@rb-handoff-20-09-source-1]

The new property type lets creators and publishers connect supported accounts and see how content from:

- Instagram;
- TikTok;
- X;
- YouTube;

performs in Google Search and Discover.

Google says platform properties can provide:

- Performance reports;
- clicks;
- impressions;
- post-level data;
- queries;
- Insights;
- Achievements;
- export capability.

The feature is rolling out gradually.

**Why this is a major SEO change.**

For years, Search Console mainly answered:

> How does my website perform in Google?

The new property answers:

> How does content I publish on another platform perform in Google?

That changes content strategy.

A brand can now see whether Google searchers discover:

- a YouTube tutorial;
- a TikTok demonstration;
- an Instagram post;
- an X thread;

before they ever visit the brand’s website.

That is first-party search demand data about off-site content.

**Who should create a platform property.**

This is useful for:

- creators without websites;
- publishers with strong social channels;
- product brands;
- local businesses;
- news organizations;
- agencies;
- video teams;
- in-house SEO teams.

If social content ranks in Google, it belongs in search measurement.

**How to add the property.**

Google’s launch instructions are straightforward.[@rb-handoff-20-09-source-1]

1. Open Search Console.
2. Open the property selector.
3. Choose **Add property**.
4. Select one of the supported platforms.
5. Follow the authorization workflow.

Supported at launch:

```text
Instagram
TikTok
X
YouTube
```

Availability is gradual.

If the option is missing, do not create a fake URL-prefix property for `instagram.com/yourbrand` and assume it is equivalent.

Wait for access.

**Performance report.**

Google says the Performance report can show:

- total clicks;
- impressions;
- other performance metrics;
- specific posts;
- queries;
- exportable data.[@rb-handoff-20-09-source-1]

This can answer:

```text
Which TikTok gets Google impressions?
Which YouTube video gets Search clicks?
Which Instagram posts surface for branded queries?
Which queries discover the account?
```

That is useful keyword research.

**Query research from social content.**

A platform property can expose terms that lead people from Google to a social post.

Use them to classify demand.

**Problem queries.**

```text
how to fix
why does
error
not working
```

Create:

- support article;
- video;
- FAQ;
- tool.

**Comparison queries.**

```text
X vs Y
best
alternative
review
worth it
```

Create decision content.

**Branded queries.**

```text
brand pricing
brand reviews
brand login
brand scam
```

Strengthen entity pages and reputation coverage.

**Visual queries.**

```text
how it looks
before after
setup
demo
```

Use video and image content.

**Connect social and website strategy.**

Do not copy the same paragraph everywhere.

Build a content system.

Example:

```text
Website:
Complete technical guide

YouTube:
10-minute demonstration

TikTok:
30-second failure example

Instagram:
Annotated visual

X:
Change announcement / expert note
```

Then link users toward the format appropriate to the next step.

Google’s announcement specifically notes growing interest in firsthand perspectives and different content formats.[@rb-handoff-20-09-source-1]

**Insights report.**

Google says platform properties can include an Insights view with:

- recent traffic trends;
- top-performing posts;
- discovery patterns.

Use this for weekly editorial review.

Ask:

- what accelerated?
- what declined?
- which platform post earned new Search visibility?
- what query cluster appeared?
- does the website already answer it?

**Achievements.**

Achievements are milestone-oriented.

They can show growth such as reaching a new threshold of Google Search clicks in the last 28 days.[@rb-handoff-20-09-source-1]

Nice for humans.

Less useful for rigorous analysis.

Do not build an executive KPI around digital confetti.

**Build a unified content inventory.**

Create one table.

```text
TOPIC
WEBSITE URL
YOUTUBE
TIKTOK
INSTAGRAM
X
PRIMARY QUERY
SEARCH CLICKS
SEARCH IMPRESSIONS
CONVERSION ROLE
```

This reveals gaps.

Example:

```text
TikTok ranks
website has no page
```

That can justify a durable site resource.

Or:

```text
website ranks
YouTube has no supporting video
```

That can justify a visual asset.

**Social posts can be search assets.**

Social content is often treated as disposable feed material.

Google platform properties make a different reality measurable:

Some posts have long-tail search life.

A YouTube tutorial from a year ago can keep receiving Search impressions.

An X post explaining a technical change can surface for a niche query.

A TikTok demonstration can become the most visible content a brand owns for a product question.

Track them like assets.

**Reporting cautions.**

Do not merge metrics blindly across platforms.

A Google Search click to YouTube and a Google Search click to your website are two different destinations.

Track:

- destination;
- query;
- content type;
- landing environment;
- conversion path.

Also preserve platform-property rollout dates.

A sudden jump from zero can mean:

```text
measurement became available
```

not:

```text
visibility exploded overnight
```

**Agency opportunity.**

Agencies can use platform properties to expand SEO reporting without pretending social metrics are rankings.

A useful monthly report can include:

- website Search performance;
- YouTube Search performance;
- TikTok Search performance;
- Instagram Search performance;
- X Search performance;
- query overlap;
- content opportunities.

This makes SEO a content-discovery discipline rather than one website dashboard.

## Failure cases

**FAQ.**

**Which platforms are supported?**

Google announced Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube at launch.

**Can I see search queries?**

Google says Performance reports can show which queries drive traffic to posts.

**Does it include Discover?**

Google says the properties help show performance in Search and Discover.

**Why can’t I add one?**

Rollout is gradual and may not have reached the account yet.

**Do I need a website?**

Google says the feature can help creators even if they do not have their own website.

**Verdict.**

Search Console platform properties are one of the most strategically interesting Search Console changes in years.

Google is acknowledging that the search result can lead to your social content, not merely your domain.

The brands that treat every format as part of one searchable knowledge system will have better data than the brands still arguing whether TikTok “counts as SEO.”
