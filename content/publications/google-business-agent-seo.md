## Preconditions

Business Agent lets shoppers chat with a brand directly on Google Search. Eligibility starts with Merchant Center, approved free listings, and a claimed brand profile, while answer quality depends on the product and business data the merchant supplies.

Google **Business Agent** is a conversational experience on Google Search that lets shoppers chat with a brand.

Google says the agent can use:

- Merchant Center product information;
- the merchant’s website;
- additional business data the merchant provides.

The experience can answer questions such as:

- What is this rug made of?
- Do these shoes run true to size?
- How many boxes of tile do I need?

For eligible U.S. ecommerce businesses, customization is available through Merchant Center.

This is not a traditional organic ranking feature.

It is a new brand interaction surface inside Search.

**Current eligibility.**

Google’s current Merchant Center help lists requirements including:

- U.S.-based ecommerce store;
- verified Merchant Center account;
- at least 50 approved free listings;
- claimed brand profile.

Availability can evolve.

Do not promise clients they can enable Business Agent without checking the account.

**Why this matters for SEO teams.**

The agent uses the merchant’s website as an information source.

That means the SEO team already controls many of the inputs:

- product descriptions;
- category pages;
- sizing guides;
- returns;
- shipping;
- materials;
- FAQs;
- support content.

The question becomes:

> If an AI assistant reads our site, will it find a clear, current, non-contradictory answer?

That is information architecture.

**Product data comes first.**

Maintain Merchant Center fields accurately.

Examples:

- title;
- brand;
- price;
- availability;
- color;
- size;
- material;
- identifiers.

A chatbot cannot reliably answer:

> Does this come in navy?

if the feed says only:

```text
Color: Blue
```

while the website says:

```text
Navy / Midnight / Deep Blue
```

Normalize product vocabulary.

**Your website trains the customer experience indirectly.**

Google says Business Agent can use website information in addition to Merchant Center.

Publish clear support pages for:

- sizing;
- compatibility;
- materials;
- shipping;
- returns;
- warranties;
- installation;
- care;
- subscriptions;
- membership.

Do not hide critical answers inside PDFs or image-only tabs.

Make the public text accessible.

**Customize the agent carefully.**

Google allows merchants to customize elements such as:

- tone of voice;
- welcome message;
- conversation starters;
- colors;
- support handoff.

Do not make the agent “funny” before making it accurate.

First goal:

```text
correct answer
```

Second:

```text
brand-consistent answer
```

A charming wrong shipping policy is still wrong.

**Conversation starters are keyword research.**

The prompts you choose reveal what you believe users want to ask.

Good starters:

- Find the right running shoe for me.
- Compare our two mattress firmness options.
- Help me calculate how many tiles I need.

Weak:

- Ask us anything!
- Explore our amazing brand!

Use task-oriented starters.

Then review actual customer questions when performance reporting becomes available.

**Support handoff is essential.**

Google lets merchants add support contact information.

Use it.

The agent should know when the answer belongs to a human.

Examples:

- damaged order;
- refund dispute;
- warranty exception;
- charge;
- personalized medical question;
- account security.

Do not let the AI improvise policy exceptions.

**Test answer accuracy.**

Create a QA set.

Categories:

```text
price
availability
materials
size
compatibility
shipping
returns
warranty
store policy
promotions
```

Ask each question in several ways.

Record:

- answer;
- source page;
- correctness;
- missing context;
- dangerous ambiguity.

Fix the source data first where possible.

**Avoid contradictory sources.**

Common problem:

```text
product page: 30-day returns
FAQ: 60-day returns
policy page: 45-day returns
```

The AI now has three plausible answers.

Create one canonical policy source.

Update dependent pages.

This is good SEO even without Business Agent.

**Brand profile identity.**

Claim and maintain the brand profile.

Keep:

- logo;
- brand name;
- website;
- Merchant Center;
- account ownership;

under business control.

Do not let an agency become the only admin of the profile.

Business Agent is a customer-facing channel.

Treat access accordingly.

**Measure the right outcomes.**

Google says performance insights are coming to Business Agent.

In the meantime, track what you can:

- support handoffs;
- product clicks;
- branded searches;
- conversions;
- Merchant Center performance.

When agent-specific reporting becomes available, keep it separate.

A chat is not a website session.

**Common failure patterns.**

- missing product attributes;
- outdated policy;
- conflicting website copy;
- support number wrong;
- product unavailable;
- customized tone too aggressive;
- conversation starters too vague;
- no human escalation;
- agency owns account.

These are governance problems disguised as AI problems.

## Failure cases

**FAQ.**

**What is Google Business Agent?**

A conversational brand experience on Google Search that answers shopper questions.

**Where do answers come from?**

Google says Merchant Center, the merchant website, and additional business data.

**Who can customize it?**

Eligible merchants meeting Google’s current criteria.

**Does Business Agent improve organic ranking?**

Google has not documented customization as a general ranking factor.

**Can I control the tone?**

Yes. Google provides customization options for supported merchants.

**Setup checklist.**

- Merchant Center verified.
- 50+ approved free listings where required.
- Brand profile claimed.
- Product data complete.
- Website policies current.
- Conflicting copy removed.
- Conversation starters task-based.
- Brand tone reviewed.
- Support handoff added.
- QA question set run.
- Account ownership business-controlled.
- Reporting plan created.

**Verdict.**

Business Agent turns website quality into a conversational customer-service problem.

If your public data is vague, contradictory, or stale, the agent inherits the mess.

The best optimization is the same thing the best ecommerce sites needed before AI: one reliable source of product and policy truth.

**Verification record.**

- Current eligibility and customization options were checked against Merchant Center Help.
- Google’s warning that Business Agent can sometimes provide inaccurate information is reflected in the QA recommendation.
- No ranking benefit is claimed.
